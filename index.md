# The Woe Chronicles — Privacy Policy

Creator: CharonIX / charonIXI

Scope: the skills-only text RPG package, version 0.9.0, supplied for use within ChatGPT.

## 1. What the plugin provides

The Woe Chronicles supplies prewritten game instructions and reference material for a fictional text RPG. It has no creator-operated server, database, external account system, analytics, advertising, or data-collection connection. No separate account with the creator is required to play.

## 2. No player information sent to the creator

The plugin provides no mechanism for transmitting player conversations, campaign saves, uploaded files, account information, contact details, or other personal information to the creator. Using this package does not send those records to CharonIX / charonIXI.

The creator does not collect, maintain, sell, or share player personal information through this plugin. There is no creator-held player database or separate creator retention period because this package does not collect that information.

## 3. Fictional characters and game state

Character names, appearances, backgrounds, relationships, inventories, and campaign events are fictional game information. Real names, addresses, contact details, identification documents, credentials, or other personal information are not required to play.

References to game memory, records, or saves mean game information used within the player's ChatGPT conversation or a checkpoint the player chooses to retain through available ChatGPT features. They do not mean that the creator operates a storage service or receives a copy of the campaign.

## 4. ChatGPT and OpenAI data handling

ChatGPT processes prompts and other content under OpenAI's applicable terms, privacy policies, and the player's account settings. This plugin's policy describes the creator's practices; it does not replace OpenAI's policies or change OpenAI's retention, training, disclosure, or access rules.

OpenAI's applicable privacy policy describes how OpenAI and authorized recipients may process information. This policy does not promise that only OpenAI employees can access data or that ChatGPT stores no information.

OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

For account data controls, deletion, privacy requests, and applicable retention information, use OpenAI's privacy resources and ChatGPT account controls. The creator cannot access or delete a player's private ChatGPT records through this plugin.

## 5. External services and optional communication

This package does not include external tools or integrations that send player information to the creator or a third-party service. Gameplay does not require joining Discord, visiting an external website, or sending the creator a private chat or save.

If a player separately chooses to share a conversation, contact someone, or use another service, that is outside the plugin's automatic operation and may disclose information to the chosen recipient under the relevant service's policies. This plugin does not initiate that communication. Do not send personal information, credentials, or private campaign records to the creator as a requirement for gameplay.

## 6. Changes to these practices

This statement covers the skills-only package described above. A future version introducing creator-operated collection, storage, analytics, or external integrations would need an updated privacy explanation before those features are introduced. Installing this version is not consent to send information to the creator through future features.

## 7. Creation credit

The entirety of The Woe Chronicles RPG was created solely using ChatGPT, through the creator's prompts and direction, across multiple versions including GPT-4.1, GPT-4.5, GPT-5.1, and other versions.
