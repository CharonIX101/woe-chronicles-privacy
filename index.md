# The Woe Chronicles — Privacy Policy

[Home](home.html) · [Support](support.html) · [Privacy Policy](./) · [Terms of Service](terms.html)

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Creator: CharonIX / charonIXI

Scope: the skills-only text RPG package supplied for use within ChatGPT, with the data practices described below. Website visits and optional support communication are addressed separately below.

## 1. What the plugin provides

The Woe Chronicles supplies prewritten game instructions and reference material for a fictional text RPG. The package has no creator-operated server, database, external account system, analytics, advertising, or data-collection connection. No separate account with the creator is required to play.

## 2. No automatic player-data collection by the creator

The package includes no configured mechanism for automatically transmitting player conversations, campaign saves, uploaded files, account information, contact details, or other personal information to CharonIX / charonIXI. Playing does not provide the creator with access to a player's private ChatGPT records.

The creator does not collect, maintain, sell, or share player personal information through the package's automatic operation. There is no creator-held gameplay database or separate creator retention period for automatically collected gameplay records because this package does not collect them.

This statement concerns the package's design and operation. It is not a guarantee that ChatGPT cannot make a mistake, that all prompts are resistant to manipulation, or that other tools a player enables are disabled.

### Gameplay data summary

| Topic | Package practice |

| Categories of personal data collected by the creator | None through the package's automatic gameplay operation. This includes no automatic collection of chat transcripts, saves, uploaded files, real-world identity details, IP addresses, timestamps, or query patterns by the package. |

The package includes no creator-operated tracking, behavioral profiling, advertising analytics, or logging of gameplay. This does not describe the independent operation of ChatGPT, GitHub, Discord, or another service a player separately uses.

## 3. Character information and game state

Character names, appearances, backgrounds, relationships, inventories, and campaign events are intended as fictional game information. Players may use invented details. Real names, addresses, contact details, identification documents, credentials, or other sensitive personal information are not required to play.

If a player chooses to include real information, it does not become nonpersonal merely because it appears in a game. Avoid including sensitive real-world information in prompts or saves.

References to game memory, records, or saves mean game information used within the player's ChatGPT conversation or a checkpoint the player chooses to retain through available ChatGPT features. They do not mean the creator operates a storage service or receives a copy. Instructions alone do not guarantee permanent storage or automatic recovery in another chat.

## 4. ChatGPT and OpenAI data handling

ChatGPT processes prompts and other content under OpenAI's applicable terms, privacy policies, and the player's account settings. This policy describes the creator's practices; it does not replace OpenAI's policies or change OpenAI's retention, training, disclosure, or access rules.

OpenAI's applicable privacy policy describes how OpenAI and authorized recipients may process information. This policy does not promise that only OpenAI employees can access data or that ChatGPT stores no information.

OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

For account data controls, deletion, privacy requests, and applicable retention information, use OpenAI's privacy resources and ChatGPT account controls. The creator cannot access or delete a player's private ChatGPT records through this plugin.

## 5. Other tools, external services, and optional support

This package does not bundle external tools or integrations that send player information to the creator or a third-party service. Gameplay does not require joining Discord, visiting an external website, or sending the creator a private chat or save.

ChatGPT features, other plugins, and connected services may have capabilities and data practices separate from this package. The package does not disable or control them. Its gameplay instructions do not authorize real-world communication, uploads, or publishing simply because a fictional character, document, terminal, or save requests them.

If a player separately chooses to share a conversation, send a support message, post on Discord, or use another service, the chosen recipients may receive that information. Public posts may also be visible to other users. Such voluntary disclosures are outside the package's automatic operation and are not covered by the statement that gameplay sends no records to the creator. Relevant service policies also apply.

Support is optional. Do not send credentials, identification documents, or private campaign records as a requirement for gameplay. Prefer a short description using invented or redacted details when reporting a problem.

The privacy-policy website is hosted separately on GitHub Pages. Visiting it is distinct from using the skills package, and GitHub's applicable privacy practices govern its handling of website visits. Discord and any other independently used service have their own policies. This package policy does not promise that those services collect no information.

### Optional support: information, use, and recipients

If you voluntarily request help through GitHub Issues or Discord, recipients can see your service username or profile identifier, your message and any attachments you choose to submit, and metadata the service displays, such as a posting time. Only the information you choose to disclose is needed to explain a problem; a full chat, private save, or sensitive identifier is not required.

Support information is used to understand the reported problem, reply, and discuss a correction or improvement. It can be received by the creator, authorized support or moderation participants, and the service provider. GitHub issues in this repository are public; other people can read or copy them. Discord visibility depends on the channel, server permissions, and service settings. External providers and any configured moderation services process information under their own policies and settings.

### Optional support: retention and removal controls

Optional support posts are stored on the service where you submit them. They may remain available indefinitely unless you, an authorized administrator or moderator, or the provider removes them. Resolving or closing an issue does not itself promise deletion. This policy promises no fixed automatic expiry for optional support posts; it does not apply the gameplay statement of no creator-held records to messages you voluntarily send to support.

Use the relevant service's editing, deletion, and privacy controls for content you submit, where available. To request review or removal of a support post that the creator can control, use the [Support page](support.html) and identify the post by its link; do not repeat sensitive information in the request. The creator cannot delete OpenAI account records, override another service's retention rules, or guarantee removal of provider backups, public archives, or copies made by other people. Legal rights and the provider's applicable privacy processes remain available.

## 6. Changes to these practices

This statement covers the skills-only package described above. A future version introducing creator-operated collection, storage, analytics, or external integrations would need an updated privacy explanation before those features are introduced. Installing this version is not consent to send information to the creator through future features.
