# The Woe Chronicles

[Home](home.html) · [Support](support.html) · [Privacy Policy](./) · [Terms of Service](terms.html)

A freeform text RPG for fictional adventures within ChatGPT.

Create a character, choose a setting, and decide what your character attempts. The game instructions guide narration, consequences, NPC relationships, factions, inventory, progression, and campaign checkpoints.

You can begin a new game, create a character, or continue from a checkpoint you provide. Continuity depends on the records and ChatGPT features actually available; permanent memory and perfect bookkeeping are not guaranteed.

## Creator

CharonIX / charonIXI, also known as CharonIX101.

The creator developed the RPG instruction and reference text documents using ChatGPT through their prompts and direction. The project claims no ownership of third-party characters, worlds, trademarks, or other media referenced during play.

## Purpose and privacy

The Woe Chronicles is intended solely for fictional entertainment. Gameplay requires no separate creator account or sensitive real-world identity information. The package includes no creator-operated gameplay data-collection connection. OpenAI's applicable policies govern ChatGPT's handling of conversations.
