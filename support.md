# The Woe Chronicles — Support

[Home](home.html) · [Support](support.html) · [Privacy Policy](./) · [Terms of Service](terms.html)

Support for The Woe Chronicles text RPG by CharonIX / charonIXI (CharonIX101).

## Get help or report a problem

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/CharonIX101/woe-chronicles-privacy/issues) to report a problem or request help. Select New issue to submit a report; a GitHub account may be required to post. Existing reports can be read publicly.

The creator's optional [Discord support server](https://discord.gg/vzdAvK5Q3) is another contact route. Discord controls access to its service. If the invite is unavailable, use GitHub Issues.

Describe what you tried, what you expected, and what happened. A short example with invented or redacted details is enough; you do not need to provide your full conversation or save. No response time is guaranteed.

## Protect your information

GitHub issues are public. Messages on external services can be visible to their recipients, moderators, or other users depending on where they are posted. Do not post passwords, identification documents, addresses, account details, or private campaign records. Information you voluntarily send for support is separate from the package's automatic operation.

Gameplay does not require using support or sharing personal information. See the [Privacy Policy](./) for the package's practices and the relevant external service's policies for that service.
